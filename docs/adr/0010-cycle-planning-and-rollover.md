# Cycles separate planning from Ticket progress

Cycles are goal-led plans within one Project: multiple may be Prepared, but only one may be Active, and each Ticket belongs to at most one non-archived Cycle at a time. Any non-archived Ticket can be planned, whatever its Status, so a Cycle can also record work that was finished before it was planned. Starting a Prepared Cycle while another is Active requires confirmation to archive the current Cycle and rolls its unfinished, non-archived Tickets into the selected Cycle, while its Done Tickets stay behind; archived Cycles retain their final membership as history. This keeps plans distinct from Ticket Status, allows the next plan to be prepared without conflicting assignments, and makes rollover deliberate while preserving unfinished work.
