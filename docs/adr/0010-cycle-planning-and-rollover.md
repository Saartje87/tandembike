# Cycles separate planning from Ticket progress

Cycles are goal-led plans within one Project: multiple may be Prepared, but only one may be Active, and each Ticket belongs to at most one non-archived Cycle at a time. Any non-archived Ticket can be planned, whatever its Status, so a Cycle can also record work that was finished before it was planned. Archiving a planned Ticket keeps it in its Cycle, so restoring it brings it back to the same plan; it can still be unplanned while archived, and archiving the Cycle treats it like any other member, by its Status. Starting a Prepared Cycle while another is Active requires confirmation to archive the current Cycle and rolls its unfinished, non-archived Tickets into the selected Cycle, while its Done Tickets stay behind. Archiving takes a Cycle's unfinished Tickets out of it, back to the Backlog, so an Archived Cycle keeps only the Done Tickets as a record of the work done in it, and those are not part of the Backlog (#69). Cycles archived before #69 may still hold unfinished Tickets; those show in the Backlog. Archiving a Cycle, directly or by rollover and by the User or an Agent, also archives each finished Epic it touched together with its children: one with at least one child, whose non-archived children are all Done, and that is itself in the Cycle or has a child there (T162). Its Done children stay in the Cycle's record, archived; restoring the Epic restores them. We chose this over hiding Done Epics from the Board, which would leave them to build up unseen, and over refusing Epics with a Done child outside the Cycle, which would leave most finished Epics live. This keeps plans distinct from Ticket Status, allows the next plan to be prepared without conflicting assignments, and makes rollover deliberate while preserving unfinished work.
